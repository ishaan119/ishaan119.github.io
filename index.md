I take things apart to understand them, then write down what I found.

Mostly language models and the systems around them — what's actually inside a
model file, why a naive inference server is slow, how MCP works at the protocol
level, and what separates people shipping real work with coding agents from
people fighting them.

Every post is a trace of something I actually ran, with the numbers I measured.
